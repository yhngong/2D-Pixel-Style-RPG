Adventurer's Guide to Valoria

Your Goal: Become the Strongest Warrior

You spawn in Oakhaven Village with 100 Gold. Visit the 4 village shops to buy your first Weapon, Pickaxe, Armor, and Potions, then explore 7 massive biomes, conquer 4 World Bosses, mine rare ores, and forge +50 Mythic Armor!

Controls & Abilities

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Walk around the 2.5D pixel world
  • Left Click / Spacebar: Swing weapon (3-hit combo + projectiles on Legendary/Mythic blades) OR mine blocks with Pickaxe
  • Right Click (or B): Place selected block from hotbar onto the map
  • Shift: Dash / Dodge roll with speed burst
  • Q Key: 360° Whirlwind AoE Slash
  • F Key: Talk to Village Shopkeepers, Bone Trader & Blacksmith
  • T Key: Teleport back to Oakhaven Village Plaza

6 Rarity Tiers, Level Requirements & Full-Set Bonuses

  • Common (Lv. 1+) — Full Set: +25 Max HP, +4 DEF
  • Uncommon (Lv. 3+) — Full Set: +55 Max HP, +12% Move Speed, +6 ATK
  • Rare (Lv. 6+) — Full Set: +110 Max HP, +18% Damage, +5 HP/s Regen
  • Epic (Lv. 10+) — Full Set: +220 Max HP, +28% Damage, +20% Crit, 12% Lifesteal
  • Legendary (Lv. 15+) — Full Set: +420 Max HP, +45% Damage, +30% Crit, +20 HP/s Regen
  • Mythic (Lv. 20+) — Full Set: +850 Max HP, +80% Damage, +40% Crit, 25% Lifesteal & Celestial Wings!
