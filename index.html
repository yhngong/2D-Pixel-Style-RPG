📜 Adventurer's Guide to Valoria

🎯 Your Goal: Become the Strongest Warrior

You begin in Oakhaven Village with 100 Gold. Visit the merchants to buy your first Weapon, Pickaxe, Armor, and Potions, then venture into the wilderness to slay monsters, mine ores, and defeat the Mythic Abyssal Dragon!

⚔️ Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Walk around the 2.5D world
  • Left Click / Spacebar: Swing weapon at monsters OR mine ore/blocks with Pickaxe
  • Right Click (or B): Place selected block from hotbar onto the ground
  • F Key: Interact with Village Shops & the Blacksmith
  • 1 - 8 Keys: Select Hotbar slot (Drink potions, open Loot Chests, select blocks)
  • I or E Key: Open Equipment & Inventory

🌈 Rarity Tiers & Level Requirements

All Weapons, Armor, and Pickaxes come in 6 rarities. Better armor requires higher Hero Levels to equip:

  • Common (Lv. 1+) — Full Set: +15 Max HP, +2 DEF
  • Uncommon (Lv. 3+) — Full Set: +35 Max HP, +12% Move Speed, +4 ATK
  • Rare (Lv. 6+) — Full Set: +75 Max HP, +15% Damage, +3 HP/s Regen
  • Epic (Lv. 10+) — Full Set: +140 Max HP, +25% Damage, +15% Crit, 10% Lifesteal
  • Legendary (Lv. 15+) — Full Set: +280 Max HP, +40% Damage, +25% Crit, +12 HP/s Regen
  • Mythic (Lv. 20+) — Full Set: +600 Max HP, +75% Damage, +35% Crit, 20% Lifesteal, Mythic Aura!

⚒️ Blacksmith Armor Upgrades (+1 to +50)

Speak to Bram the Blacksmith in the village center to upgrade any piece of armor up to +50 using Gold! Every upgrade level increases the armor's Defense and bonus HP.

📦 Monster Drops & Chests

Monsters drop Gold, Bones, and rarely Tiered Treasure Chests based on the monster's difficulty! Stronger monsters drop higher-tier chests containing Rare, Epic, Legendary, or even Mythic gear.
